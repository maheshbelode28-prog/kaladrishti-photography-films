Terms & Policies

Everything clear before we begin

  1. Booking Advance: 50% advance payment of the booking amount is mandatory to confirm your date.
  2. Payment Schedule: Editing and work begin only after a minimum of 75% payment is completed. Final delivery is made only after 100% full payment.
  3. Cancellation: If a booking is cancelled for any reason, the advance amount will not be refunded.
  4. Soft Copy Delivery: All soft copies will be delivered within 8 days after the shoot is completed.
  5. Digital Leather Box Album (12×18 inches): ₹350 per page — up to 6 photos per page (custom page count). Album delivered within 15 days after your photo selection.
  6. Data Safety: Being electronic media, data may get corrupted, deleted or damaged due to technical reasons, and recovery may not always be possible. Clients are advised to keep their own backup from time to time.
  7. Travel Policy: FREE travel setup within a 125 km radius of Amravati. Additional travel charges apply beyond 125 km.
  8. Extra Charges (per day):
    • Traditional Photo — ₹6,000/- per day
    • Traditional Video — ₹6,000/- per day
    • Candid Photo — ₹6,000/- per day
    • Cinematography — ₹12,000/- per day
    • Drone Camera — ₹5,000/- per day
    • LED Screen — In City ₹6,000/- · Out of City ₹9,000/- per day
